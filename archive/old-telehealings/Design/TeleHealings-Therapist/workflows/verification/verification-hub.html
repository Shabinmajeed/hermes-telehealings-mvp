Consents & Ethics Agreement

Please review and agree to the Telehealings platform ethics policy.

1. Confidentiality: Therapists must maintain patient confidentiality and protect privacy in all communications, ensuring strict adherence to HIPAA and relevant local guidelines.

2. Professional Boundaries: Therapists must not engage in dual relationships or inappropriate conduct with any patients assigned through the platform.

3. Competence & Care: Practitioners agree to only provide services within their specialized areas of expertise, maintaining high standards of care.

4. Emergency Protocols: In the event of a severe clinical emergency, therapists are obligated to guide the client to local emergency services.

5. Platform Conduct: All communications, scheduling, and billing must remain within the Telehealings application to ensure transparency and proper logging.

1. Client Privacy: I agree to hold all information shared by clients in strict confidence, both during and after the termination of the therapeutic relationship.

2. Data Protection: I will ensure that all digital and physical records are stored securely and in compliance with applicable data protection laws (e.g., HIPAA or local equivalents).

3. Platform Usage: I understand that session notes and communications within the Telehealings platform are encrypted and must not be exported or shared via unauthorized third-party channels.

4. Exceptions: I am aware of the legal and ethical exceptions to confidentiality, including imminent risk of harm to the client or others, and mandatory reporting laws regarding abuse.
